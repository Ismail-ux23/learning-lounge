PRACTICE · PREDICT THE OUTPUTExample

Small details make a difference.

total = 0
for number in range(1, 4):
    total += number
print(total)
See the explanation

The range includes 1, 2, and 3. Adding them gives 6. The stop value, 4, is excluded.
